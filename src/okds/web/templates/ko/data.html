Data Skeleton

A ready-made structure for partner data. The project doesn't have the IMPACT partner data yet, so this defines what the data should look like and how it flows: upload → automatic checks (including a privacy screen) → a person reviews the batch → accepted records are summarized and linked to the Knowledge Objects.

Skeleton. It ships with made-up sample data only. Real records must be de-identified and shared under a data agreement (e.g. an IRB-approved data use agreement) before upload.

Before you upload

  • De-identify first. People are study codes (P-0001), ages are bands, time is “day N” since the rehabilitation started, years only. No names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, record numbers or full dates.
  • Have permission. Real patient-level records need an approved data agreement (and usually IRB approval). If in doubt, upload summaries or use the synthetic sample.
  • Only the model's columns. Extra columns are refused, so nothing identifying slips in.
  • Automatic checks: required fields, types and ranges, allowed values, links between tables, KO links to real topics of the right type, duplicate codes, and a privacy screen for emails, phone numbers, SSNs, dates, record numbers, web and street addresses, and exact ages.
  • Then a person reviews the batch in the Review queue. Only accepted batches are used. Summaries hide groups smaller than 5.
  • Uploaded records are stored in data/records/ on this computer and are not committed to git.
