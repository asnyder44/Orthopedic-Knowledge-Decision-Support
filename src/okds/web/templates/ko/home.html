What is a Knowledge Object?

A short, trusted note about one orthopedic topic. Every fact has a source, and it links to related topics.

Here is a real KO, shortened, with its 4 parts numbered.

1
LabelsID, type, reader, version. The same on every KO.
2
ExplanationPlain words about one topic. Every fact ends with a source marker.
3
SourcesWhere each fact comes from. At least 2 trusted ones.
4
LinksNamed connections to other KOs, so people and computers can follow them.
