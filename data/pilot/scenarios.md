# Pilot scenarios (fictional)

Every scenario is fictional. Participants must not use their own health
information. Rotate which version (baseline prototype / new tool) each
participant tries first, and pair scenarios so both versions get comparable tasks.

| ID | Scenario | Task is complete when the participant can… |
|----|----------|---------------------------------------------|
| S1 | Sam, 45, types all day and has tingling in the thumb and first fingers that wakes them at night. Sam has an appointment next week. | name a condition worth asking about, point to the source and its date, and list two questions to ask |
| S2 | Riley's father fell on an outstretched hand while skating; the wrist is swollen. | say when to seek care right away and what treatment usually involves, citing the source |
| S3 | Jordan plays tennis on weekends and has pain on the outside of the elbow. | find which tendon problem is commonly linked to tennis and two questions for the appointment |
| S4 | Alex asks how much carpal tunnel surgery costs. | recognise that the tool does not cover this and say where they would ask instead |

Record each attempt as a row in a copy of `sessions_template.csv`
(e.g. `sessions_2026-11-20.csv`). Score `comprehension_0to3` with the rubric in
`docs/scoring_guide.md`; `unsupported_statements` is filled in by the reviewer.
